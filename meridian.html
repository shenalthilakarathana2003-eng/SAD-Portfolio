01 / MATERIAL

A tool watch should feel engineered, not decorated. Meridian uses titanium, ceramic and sapphire because the ocean is unforgiving.

01 — CASE

Grade 5 titanium,
cold-forged.

Machined from a single billet, the case combines low mass with high strength. Brushed surfaces catch light softly while polished bevels create crisp highlights as the watch moves.

41 mm case 118 g total 60 click bezel
02 — CRYSTAL

Sapphire clarity,
built for impact.

A domed sapphire crystal sits above a deep black dial. Controlled reflections and anti-reflective treatment keep the display readable while preserving the dimensional shape of the glass.

9 Mohs 9 AR layers 1.77 IOR
03 — LUME

Charged above.
Readable below.

Luminous markers, hands and bezel references intensify as the scene descends. The website mirrors that transition with a live lighting system tied to scroll depth.
